1. Privacy Policy

Hum aapki privacy ka poora khayal rakhte hain. Jab aap Dukaan par account banate hain, hum aapka naam, email, phone number, aur delivery address collect karte hain — sirf order process karne aur aapse contact karne ke liye.

Hum aapki personal jaankari kisi third-party ko bina aapki permission ke nahi bechte. Payment information (card/UPI details) hum khud store nahi karte — ye seedha humare payment partner (Razorpay) ke secure system se guzarti hai.

Aap kabhi bhi apna account data dekhne, update karne, ya delete karwane ki request kar sakte hain — hume contact karke.

2. Terms & Conditions

Dukaan use karke, aap in terms ko accept karte hain:

  • Aapko sahi aur poori jaankari deni hogi (naam, address, phone) taaki order sahi se deliver ho sake.
  • Prices aur stock availability bina notice ke badal sakte hain.
  • Hum kisi bhi order ko cancel karne ka adhikar rakhte hain agar fraud ya galat jaankari ka shak ho.
  • Website ka misuse (jaise fake orders, spam) account suspend karne ka kaaran ban sakta hai.

3. Refund Policy

Agar aapka order cancel ho jata hai ya return approve hota hai, refund process is tarah hota hai:

  • Online payment: Refund 5-7 business days me aapke original payment method me wapas aa jayega.
  • Cash on Delivery: Refund aapke diye gaye bank account me 7-10 business days me transfer hoga.
  • Refund process hone se pehle product ka return/inspection complete hona zaroori hai.

4. Return Policy

Aap delivery ke 7 din ke andar return request kar sakte hain, agar:

  • Product damaged ya defective aaya ho
  • Galat product bheja gaya ho (jo order kiya wo nahi mila)
  • Product apni original packaging me, bina use kiye ho

Kuch products (jaise personal care items, khaane-peene ki cheezein) hygiene reasons se return nahi ho sakte, jab tak defective na hon.

5. Shipping Policy

Order confirm hone ke baad, hum usse process karke aapke diye gaye address par bhejte hain. Delivery time area ke hisaab se alag ho sakta hai (aam taur par 3-7 business days).

Delivery charges (agar lagu ho) order ke time hi dikhaye jaate hain — koi hidden charge nahi hota. Kuch minimum order amount ke upar free delivery bhi mil sakti hai.

6. Seller (Supplier) Policy

Dukaan par product bechne ke liye:

  • Supplier ko apna GST number aur business details register karte waqt dena hoga.
  • Admin verification ke baad hi supplier product add kar sakta hai.
  • Product ka final selling price sirf Dukaan admin/team decide karta hai — supplier sirf suggested price de sakta hai.
  • Supplier ko order aane par time par product ready/dispatch karna hoga.
  • Payment settlement delivery ke 7 din baad hota hai.

7. Supplier Agreement

Jab koi supplier Dukaan par register karta hai, wo in baaton se sehmat hota hai:

  • Sirf genuine, quality products hi list karega — koi nakli ya galat jaankari wala product nahi.
  • Diye gaye stock number accurate honge, taaki over-selling na ho.
  • Dukaan platform ki policies (pricing, GST, returns) follow karega.
  • Agar koi supplier fraud ya baar-baar quality issues karta hai, uska account suspend kiya ja sakta hai.

8. Cookie Policy

Dukaan website chalane ke liye kuch basic browser storage (jaise login token) use karti hai, taaki aapko baar-baar login na karna pade. Hum tracking cookies ka use advertising ke liye nahi karte.

9. Data Protection Policy

Aapka data secure servers par store hota hai, encrypted passwords ke saath (kabhi bhi plain text me nahi rakha jata). Hum industry-standard security practices follow karte hain (jaise rate-limiting, secure authentication) taaki aapki jaankari safe rahe.

Data breach ki sthiti me, hum applicable law ke hisaab se affected users ko turant inform karenge.

10. Warranty Policy

Product warranty (agar applicable ho) manufacturer/brand ke terms ke hisaab se hoti hai, jo product listing me mention rahegi. Warranty claims ke liye aapko purchase invoice/order number ki zaroorat hogi.

Dukaan khud koi extra warranty provide nahi karta jab tak specifically na likha ho — hum sirf supplier aur customer ke beech ka madhyam hain.

Koi sawaal ho to hume contact karein.
